# Condensed deck (about 10 slides): the version clients approve

Same evidence as the long deck, one slide per argument. Hard cap 15; aim for about 10 including the cover
and Q&A. A default running order:

1. **Cover**
2. **Executive summary** - the readiness index out of 100 with its five dimensions, the three biggest
   gaps, and the top owned priority. If the client needs the "why now" case, put one sourced stat here
   rather than spending a slide on it.
3. **The buyer** (required) - who they are in one line, the search and AI engines they over- or
   under-use, the sites and communities they trust, and what that means for the engines tracked and the
   off-site targets. From `audience-research.md`.
4. **AI answers today** - the share-of-voice scoreboard across N non-branded prompts, led by where the
   client loses, with the one insight that explains it (frequency vs position, a trust cluster with no owned
   answer, or open goals nobody claims).
5. **Where the answers come from** - sources by type with cite rate and client presence, and the top
   three off-site plays ranked from that table.
6. **The competitive field** - the tiered competitor table, the brand vs non-brand reading, and the Pareto
   stat in the form "N pages carry 80% of CompetitorX's traffic", with what it implies for the number of
   pages the client actually needs.
7. **Topics to own** - the topical authority theme table cut to the top themes, with verdicts and next
   moves (the chart goes to the long deck).
8. **What wins on the page** - page-type economics, the winning-page skeleton, the build order and the
   schema line.
9. **The way forward** - content decisions and the calendar first, then the same-week technical fixes, the
   phases, how progress is measured, and what is needed from the client.
10. **Q&A**

## How to condense from the long deck

- Keep every evidence table; cut prose, never data.
- Fold the education material into one stat on the executive summary.
- Merge glossary, hub and schema strategy into the single "what wins" slide.
- Fold the technical module into numbered same-week fixes on the way-forward slide.
- Each slide carries one claim, one table or visual, and one bold insight sentence.
- Run `data/dedupe_audit.py` after the cut: merging is where duplicates appear.
