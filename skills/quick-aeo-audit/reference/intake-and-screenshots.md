# Intake and reading client screenshots

Inputs vary. You may get screenshots from the client's prompt-tracking tool, an
existing deck to update, a competitive-analysis document, or only a domain and a
competitor list. When screenshots exist, most of what you need to scope the
audit is in them: open them as images and read them.

## What each screenshot can give you

- Overview / AI-visibility dashboard: the client's branded-inclusive visibility
  (N%), its market position (#K of the tracked brands), per-platform visibility
  (Perplexity / Gemini / Claude / ChatGPT), and usually a leaderboard card. The
  leaderboard IS the competitor set.
- Share-of-voice chart: the split of mentions across the tracked brands. Check
  that the brands on it are the client's real competitors (`gotchas.md` #6).
- Prompt list (top performing / low visibility): the client's existing tracked
  prompts. "Top performing" are usually branded prompts ("<brand> reviews",
  "<brand> vs <alternative>"). "Low visibility / not found" are the category
  prompts they lose: your seed list for the real prompt universe.

## Also collect

- A client site scrape: positioning, products and differentiators, segments
  served, proof points (retention rate, customer logos, certifications, awards).
  This feeds the narrative-alignment slide (what the client says about itself
  vs what AI says about it) and the branded-prompt set.
- The competitive-analysis document, if there is one. Use its competitor
  identities and the client's counter-narrative for the versus and
  narrative-alignment framing.

## Reconcile the two visibility numbers; never let them contradict

A dashboard number usually includes branded prompts, so it is high (N%, ranked
near the top). Your non-branded baseline will usually be lower (M% share of
voice, ranked lower). That gap is the whole story: strong on branded, weak on
category. State both numbers explicitly on the share-of-voice slide and in its
speaker notes, and say why they differ.

## Deck size

If the client supplied a template, keep its existing slides, then ADD insight
slides (charts plus the prompt-performance table) in the analysis section.
Without a template, follow the outline in `deck-layout.md` (about a dozen
slides). A data-dense deck is expected: do not pad with filler, but do add real
charts.
