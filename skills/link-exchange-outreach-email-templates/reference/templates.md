# Link exchange email templates

Contents: Voice · What makes these emails work · T1 Hand-off (the owner brings in a teammate) · T2 Ask for URLs and fits (teammate's first reply) · T3 Counter with other pages (target page is frozen or not a fit) · T4 Decline a competitor listing (a vendor in our category wants a place on our page) · T5 Ask for the full map (3-way exchange) · T6 Accept and agree the swap · T7 Swap live URLs and close · T8 Nudge (they went quiet) · T9 Outbound proposal (we start it) · T10 Polite no (not a competitor, just not a fit) · Third-party correction requests

Placeholders are in [square brackets]. Fill all of them before sending. The wording below is a neutral
default; adapt it to the client's own voice and email conventions if they have them.

## Voice

- One named person writes in the first person ("I") and addresses the reader as "you". Keep "we" and "our"
  for the company's pages and product.
- Start by saying who you are and your role in a single sentence.
- Refer to their page by its title so they know you actually looked at it.
- Make one clear ask per email, and say what they get in return.
- Keep it short: most replies fit in three to five sentences.
- End with a question they can answer with yes or no.
- No em dashes.
- Signature: `[Name], [Role], [Brand]`, or the client's own sign-off if they have one.

## What makes these emails work

- A short, specific introduction beats a long company pitch.
- Present the swap in terms of what each site's readers gain, not what each site gains in rankings.
- Offer to let the other side adjust your suggested copy so it fits their style; it removes friction.
- A closing yes/no question gets more replies than an open "let me know your thoughts".

## T1 Hand-off (the owner brings in a teammate)

> Hi [first name],
>
> Adding [teammate] to this thread. They look after our partnerships and will follow up with next steps.
>
> Thanks,
>
> [Name], [Role], [Brand]

## T2 Ask for URLs and fits (teammate's first reply)

Also the first reply when nobody handed the thread over: drop the "[owner] asked me" clause, and leave out
any line they already answered (for example, if they named our page, ask only about theirs).

> Hi [first name],
>
> I'm [Name], [role] at [Brand], and [owner] asked me to pick this up. So I can check what fits, could you
> send:
>
> - The page or pages on [brand domain] you're thinking of
> - The URL of the page on your site where [Brand] would be mentioned
> - Any of our pages you think your readers would find useful
>
> Thanks,
>
> [Name], [Role], [Brand]

## T3 Counter with other pages (target page is frozen or not a fit)

> Hi [first name],
>
> Thanks for the details. [page title] isn't open for changes at the moment, so I can't add anything there.
> These two pages may be a better match for your readers anyway:
>
> - [Page 1 title]: [URL]
> - [Page 2 title]: [URL]
>
> If either works, tell me which page on your site you'd link from and the sentence you'd suggest, and I'll
> review it.
>
> Thanks,
>
> [Name], [Role], [Brand]

If you have no pages ready to offer yet, leave out the list and ask which of your pages they think would
fit.

## T4 Decline a competitor listing (a vendor in our category wants a place on our page)

Use when a company that sells what we sell asks for a place on one of our pages (a list, roundup, directory
or resource page), sends a drafted entry for one, or asks for a link from one of our pages.

> Hi [first name],
>
> Thanks for reaching out. As a rule we don't feature or link to companies in our own category on our
> pages, so I'll pass on [page title].
>
> If you're ever writing about [topic] and want an outside view, I'd be glad to contribute a comment.
>
> Thanks,
>
> [Name], [Role], [Brand]

## T5 Ask for the full map (3-way exchange)

> Hi [first name],
>
> Thanks for the follow-up. Before I can say yes or no to a 3-way arrangement, I need to see:
>
> - All three domains and who runs each one
> - The specific page on each site, and which site links to which
> - Whether any link is paid or marked as sponsored
>
> With that in hand I can give you a quick answer.
>
> Thanks,
>
> [Name], [Role], [Brand]

## T6 Accept and agree the swap

> Hi [first name],
>
> Happy to go ahead. Here's what I understand each side will do:
>
> - [Their domain]: mention [Brand] with a link to [our URL], on [their page URL], in the main text near
>   the part about [topic].
> - [Our domain]: mention [their brand] with a link to [their URL], on [our page URL], in the section on
>   [topic].
>
> I plan to publish ours on [date]. Could you send me your URL once your change is live? I'll do the same.
>
> Thanks,
>
> [Name], [Role], [Brand]

## T7 Swap live URLs and close

> Hi [first name],
>
> Our side is now live: [our live URL]. Thanks for working on this with us. If anything needs adjusting on
> your end, just tell me.
>
> Thanks,
>
> [Name], [Role], [Brand]

## T8 Nudge (they went quiet)

> Hi [first name],
>
> Following up on [the pages / the swap] we discussed on [date]. No rush; I'm ready whenever you are.
>
> Thanks,
>
> [Name], [Role], [Brand]

Link-changed variant (their link was removed or moved after the swap went live). Replace the middle line
with:

> I noticed the link to [our URL] on [their page URL] is [no longer on the page / now in the footer]. We
> agreed on a mention in the main text, and ours is still live at [our live URL]. Could you put it back?

## T9 Outbound proposal (we start it)

> Hi [first name],
>
> I'm [Name], [role] at [Brand]. I came across "[their page title]" and noticed [one specific, true,
> checked fact about it, e.g. that it is cited as a source in AI answers to questions about [topic]].
>
> Our page "[our page title]" ([our URL]) goes into [related topic], and a link between the two could be
> useful to readers of both. I'd be glad to point to your article from our section on [topic] in return.
>
> Would that interest you?
>
> Thanks,
>
> [Name], [Role], [Brand]

## T10 Polite no (not a competitor, just not a fit)

> Hi [first name],
>
> Thanks for thinking of us. It isn't the right fit for our content at the moment, but I'll remember your
> site when we plan new pages on [topic].
>
> Thanks,
>
> [Name], [Role], [Brand]

## Third-party correction requests

These templates are not for asking a site to fix how it describes the brand. Use a skill installed for
that if there is one; otherwise tell the user the request is out of scope for this skill and stop.
